CAVEATS (printed because these numbers will be quoted):
  * IN-SAMPLE. Our book exists only where a wallet already cleared every gate, so a floor's
    'removed PnL' is measured on the legs we actually took — not on what the floor would have
    traded instead (no counterfactual book).
  * A floor also removes VOLUME, which is the input to the C-200 phase ladder (the ladder's goal
    is daily PnL, so a floor that raises edge%% while cutting cost can still slow the ladder).
  * Our as-of-entry depth is a LOWER BOUND on the wallet's true record: our observation window
    starts 2026-07-13, so a wallet that traded heavily before that reads thin.
  * Nothing here is a rule. Any floor is a separate proposal with its own pre-registered gate.
