## 1) AKCodez / prediction-market-alpha-playbook

### What it is
MIT docs-only repository (~2.6k lines, no code): a retrospective operator playbook for Polymarket+Kalshi bots. 20 edge categories (EDGES.md), a strategy-graduation methodology (METHODOLOGY.md), one deep-dive (NEG_RISK_NO_CARRY.md), ~20 operational failure modes (ANTIPATTERNS.md), plus API/architecture/step-by-step scaffolding (APIS.md, ARCHITECTURE.md, PLAYBOOK.md). Self-described as "generic instructional content," evidence tiers S/A/B/C/F with Wilson-lb95 discipline. Author anonymous; every quantitative claim is **unverified** and is treated as a hypothesis source only.

### Verified content-or-machinery (with file paths)
Read fully: EDGES.md, METHODOLOGY.md, NEG_RISK_NO_CARRY.md, ANTIPATTERNS.md. Skimmed: APIS.md, ARCHITECTURE.md (incl. Safety Guards §4, Arb-Class Tagging §7, Kelly sizing §8, Hot Config §9), README.md.
- `EDGES.md` — 20 edges w/ evidence tiers. Novel mechanism claims: (1) NEG_RISK sum-arb when ΣYES-asks > 1.0+threshold w/ **partial-basket trap** if any leg ask=null; (4) Kalshi ladder monotonicity arb w/ **NO-ask formula trap** (`1 − yes_ask ≠ NO ask`; real NO ask = `1 − yes_bid`); (6) clear-win convergence (90–95¢→99¢) — **category trap**: works sports/weather, failed commodities/macro via oracle-feed divergence; (8) sports final-score sniping via sports WS (`wss://sports-api.polymarket.com/ws`) with **slug/outcome-index gotcha** (`{sport}-{away}-{home}-{date}`, outcomes[0]=AWAY); (9/12/13) oracle-divergence FADE family + "directional gate inversion" (≥80¢ NORM wins, <80¢ loses — N=8, 100% WR claim); (10/11) insider-cluster & whale-following w/ elite-wallet filters + **news-herd vs sports-cluster taxonomy** (HERD_THRESHOLD=25 wallets) + **sibling-market discriminator** to separate basket arbers from directional insiders; (20) large-fill orderflow copy (≥$20K fills, ≥3 in 5min).

- `ANTIPATTERNS.md` — side/token alignment (outcomeIndex not strings), NO-ask formula, journal-PnL fiction (+$22K fake wins on 25 rows claim), CoinGecko-429 silent kill + **probe-diagnosis pattern** ("production 0 fires, probe finds 4 in 15s ⇒ data pipeline broken"), proxy-wallet vs EOA, microshare floor-to-4-decimals, JS float rounding, **thin-book exit cost = walk the bid ladder** (17pp+ spread = abandoned-market fingerprint), thin-book = capital lock (40–70% of mid recoverable), CRLF env trim, CI silent deploy drift, shared-DB ID collision, public Polygon RPC unreliability, **gamma `_q=` free-text search broken** ("GTA VI" catch-all), fat-tail strategies not killable on N<30, insider-vs-herd, clear-win category bias, VPS-runs-yesterday's-code.
- `APIS.md` — endpoint/gotcha catalog (data-api `/activity?user=` for resolved-market replay; positions `curPrice=0`+`redeemable=true` = LOST not win; Kalshi fee `0.07·p·(1−p)`, round-trip ~450bps ⇒ cross-venue raw edge ≥400bps else net-negative; `orderbook_fp` float-strings; sports `close_time` padded +2y → use `expected_expiration_time`).

### Testable-hypothesis / transfer list (with effort estimate)
1. **Whale-copy quality filters → C-200 lane (LOW effort, high fit).** H10/H11 heuristics: copy only wallets w/ pf ≥2.0, maxDD <25%, N≥100 trades; require ≥2 elite wallets converging same market+side ≥$10K net within 60 min; cap copy size small. Shadow-test wallet-quality thresholds against keyless leaderboard data-api (C-200 already profiles wallets — this adds *convergence-count* and *directional-consistency* gates). Effort: ~0.5–1 day incl. a tape-based cohort table.
2. **Sports-cluster vs news-herd discriminator (LOW effort).** H: per-(market,side) wallet counts ≤~13 w/ 3–8× ROI cluster = informed (copy), ≥25 = herd (skip); plus **sibling-market arb-basket exclusion** — wallet with positions across siblings (same event end_ts ±48h) is a basket arber, not a directional signal. Directly shadow-testable in C-200 w/ leaderboard+market metadata. Effort: ~1 day.
3. **Directional-gate / cohort-stratify-before-kill (LOW–MED effort, doctrinal fit).** H: aggregate WR hides sub-cohort alpha; stratify by entry-price bucket (≥80¢ NORM vs <80¢ FADE), dow, utc-window, elapsed-fraction before judging any strategy. The FADE-variant rule maps onto the v39 engine as: when signal source ≠ resolution oracle, test inverse cohort. Contradiction-check w/ stack doctrine: playbook cohort search uses point PnL/WR on raw trades — adopt only the *structure* (dimensions, NORM/FADE, lb95 gate) on the PMA tape; do not treat discovered cohorts as confirmed (playbook itself says fresh OOS required). Effort: 2–4 days to port the 74-dim cohort search to tape DB; reuse as pre-kill check.
4. **Oracle-divergence as a category (MED effort).** H: 5-min crypto Up/Down scanner-direction (Binance tick) resolves against a different oracle feed, NORM ~7% WR vs FADE ~93% (claimed N=28, lb95 77.4% — **unverified claim**). Shadow-test on PMA tape: does Binance-close direction predict Polymarket 5-min up/down resolution? If not, inversion cohort is a candidate prior feature — but per doctrine it stays a PRIOR/feature test, never standalone evidence. Effort: ~1 day tape query + report.
5. **NEG_RISK long-tail NO carry study (MED effort).** Structural (not probabilistic) edge: buy NO at ≥0.97 on consensus-loser legs of genuine neg-risk baskets (≥5 siblings, ΣYES sanity, ≤7d to resolution, per-event caps, hold to resolution, no stop). Paper-only fits stack perfectly; test on live negRisk universe + tape where resolvable. Watch: category exclusions (bins, exact-scores), thin-book at the extreme, cadence is low. Effort: 1–2 days scanner + shadow lane; sizing flat per doctrine.
6. **PnL-integrity antipatterns → adopt as invariants (LOW effort, already partly doctrine).** Data-API reconciliation with suspect flags, exit-stamping honesty (no PnL without `actual_fill_price`), formula-shaped-PnL fingerprint, tail decomposition before graduation. Port as shadow-lane invariants where the stack tracks realized fills. Effort: trivial for TS runtime where fills are real; main value = audit checklist.
7. **Execution gotchas checklist (LOW effort).** outcomeIndex-not-string, NO ask = 1−yes_bid, microshare floor-to-4, thin-book exit = walk bids, gamma free-text search broken, trim env, per-process ID prefixes. Several already known to stack; adopt as code-review checklist. Effort: <0.5 day.
8. **Clear-win convergence scanner (MED effort, lower priority).** Resolution-window sniping (0–24h, fact-verified, fire only <0.97) — only sports/weather categories per doc; commodity/macro FAILED (oracle divergence). Candidate Data-lane experiment on resolved-market repricing latency. Effort: 1–2 days to measure repricing lag on tape before any signal work.
9. **Cross-venue / structural arbs (NOT NOW).** Sum-arb, monotonicity, cross-exchange (≥400bps floor) — real but execution-tied, out of scope for a paper research stack unless a shadow study on negRisk ΣYES>1.03 occurrence rate is wanted (effort 1 day, tape-only).

## 2) 0xrsydn / polymarket-crypto-toolkit

### What it is
A real Python 3.13 monorepo (~12.3k LOC) evolved from a "BTC 5-min streak bot" into a composable toolkit: uv workspace w/ packages/{core,data,indicators,strategies,backtest,executor} plus legacy root scripts (copybot.py, copybot_v2.py, bot.py, backtest_engine.py). Vibe-coded (README admits: OpenClaw agent "Dexter" wrote it). **License status: NO LICENSE file present** (README footer claims MIT, but no LICENSE file in the clone → all-rights-reserved default). Concept donor only — do not copy code. Domain: BTC/ETH 5-min up/down crypto markets on Polymarket + Binance-candle backtesting.

### Verified content-or-machinery (with file paths)
- `src/strategies/copytrade.py` (142 ln) — CopytradeMonitor: polls `data-api.polymarket.com/activity?user=<wallet>` for each tracked wallet, regex-filters slug `^btc-updown-5m-(\d+)$`, extracts market_ts from slug, emits CopySignal (side/price/size/usdc/tx).
- `src/strategies/copytrade_ws.py` (516 ln) — HybridCopytradeMonitor: **honest stub architecture** — per-wallet detection over WS is NOT implemented (`_handle_trade` body is literally `pass`; comment admits market WS doesn't expose trader addresses); actual detection = REST polling at 1–2s, with WS trade events on the btc-updown slugs only *triggering an immediate poll* (300ms cooldown), plus Polygonscan tx enrichment.
- `src/strategies/selective_filter.py` (61 ln) + defaults in `src/config.py` — SelectiveFilter gates on: copy_delay ≤20,000ms; **fill price band 0.55–0.80**; price move ≤15%; spread ≤0.025; volatility factor <1.25; depth-at-best ≥$5. Off by default (`SELECTIVE_FILTER=false`).
- `src/core/polymarket.py` `DelayImpactModel` — impact% = √delay_s × 0.8 × liq_factor(0.5–2.0 by size/(0.5·depth_best)) × vol_factor(spread/0.02), capped 10% ("0.3%/s of delay" claim in copybot.py help; sqrt model gives 0.8% at 1s).
- `copybot.py` / `copybot_v2.py` — decision loop: skip SELLs (copy BUYs only), one copy per (wallet, market_ts), market open/accepting checks, pre-computed execution estimate by walking the book (fill_pct, slippage vs best), bankruptcy stop when bankroll < MIN_BET, daily bet/loss caps, state persistence, ws-triggered polling, circuit breaker + rate limiter (120 req/min) + structured logging. **No wallet-ranking/scoring anywhere — wallets are manually curated** ("find profitable BTC 5-min traders on polymarket.com/activity").
- `src/strategies/streak.py` (114 ln) — BTC 5-min streak-reversal signal: detect run of same-direction outcomes, bet AGAINST streak ≥ trigger=4; REVERSAL_RATES = {2: 0.540, 3: 0.579, 4: 0.667, 5: 0.824}, claimed "from 570-market backtest (2 days)" — **unverified claim, no ledger/CI artifact**. Confidence = reversal rate used directly in `kelly_size` (fraction 0.25, odds 2.0 fixed).
- `packages/strategies/src/polymarket_algo/strategies/streak_reversal.py` (27 ln) — package version of same math; **verified math**: direction = sign(close.diff()); streak = run-length via groupby(direction != shift).cumcount()+1; signal −1 (bet down) when up-streak ≥ trigger, +1 when down-streak ≥ trigger; flat size. Deterministic and correct as coded.
- `backtest_engine.py` + `packages/backtest/.../engine.py` (112/128 ln) — vectorized backtest on Binance candles: fixed buy at 0.50, win payout 0.95 (5¢ fee baked in), win = next-candle close direction; metrics WR/PnL/maxDD/Sharpe; parameter_sweep grid; walk_forward_split(0.75) — single train/test split, NOT rolling.
- `src/core/polymarket_ws.py` (896 ln) — market-channel WS client: orderbook snapshots/deltas cache, last_trade_price events, ~100ms latency claim.
- `PRD.md`/`README.md` — architecture intent; candle_direction strategy (EMA/MACD/RSI confluence on 1h/4h) targets "55%+ OOS WR" as an unvalidated PRD goal.

### Testable-hypothesis / transfer list (with effort estimate)
1. **Selective-filter gate set → C-200 shadow test (LOW effort, direct fit).** H: copies whose (a) fill price falls outside 0.55–0.80 (near 0.50 coin-flips or ≥0.85 priced-in), (b) delay >20s, (c) price move vs target's print >15%, (d) spread >2.5pp, (e) best-level depth <$5, have worse realized EV than in-band copies. Re-run their exact gate defaults as cohorts on C-200/tape data before tuning. Note the fill-band hypothesis mirrors playbook's directional-gate-inversion idea from an independent codebase — worth flagging as convergent. Effort: ~1 day.
2. **Copy-delay impact model (LOW–MED effort, concept only).** H: expected adverse move ∝ √(delay_s) scaled by order-vs-depth and spread (their coefficients 0.8/cap-10% are arbitrary). Testable on tape: realized move between target trade_ts and copy time vs delay, by depth/spread bucket → re-estimate own coefficients; use as an EV haircut before firing copies (doctrine-compatible: it's a cost model, not evidence). Effort: 1–2 days on tape.
3. **BUY-only copy + one-per-(wallet,market) dedupe (LOW effort).** Simplification choice, not evidence; testable: does including whale SELL/exits improve or hurt (their stance: ignore)? Cheap cohort on C-200 history. Effort: <1 day.
4. **Streak-reversal rate table → tape shadow test (MED effort).** H: P(direction flip | k consecutive same-direction 5-min resolutions) rises with k (54/58/67/82% claimed). Cleanly testable on PMA tape crypto up/down history with real N; also test trigger ∈ {3,4,5} and the fixed-0.50-entry assumption (real entries drift; their backtest ignores this — a known flaw to correct). If rate table survives, it is a *prior* feature candidate for v39, not evidence. Effort: 1–2 days.
5. **Latency architecture concept (LOW effort, likely redundant).** WS-triggered immediate poll (vs pure REST polling) + market-cache orderbook precompute: infra concept the stack likely already has; if C-200 still polls REST-only, the trigger-poll pattern is the one transferable piece. Effort: N/A unless gap confirmed.
6. **Paper-realistic simulation features (LOW effort, reference).** Fees ~2.5% at 50¢, spread from live book, slippage by ladder walk, delay impact — sanity-check list for the stack's paper lane (mostly already doctrine).

### NOT-usable list
- **All code** (no license file despite README MIT claim; all-rights-reserved).
- Per-wallet WebSocket trade detection — does not exist (stub).
- Their backtest as-is — fixed 50¢ entry + fixed 0.95 payout + next-Binance-candle proxy ≈ real market only when price ≈0.50; no entry-spread/slippage modeling; single 75/25 walk-forward split is weak.
- REVERSAL_RATES used as Kelly p — uncalibrated confidence; conflicts with calibrated-probability discipline (use only as prior-feature input after tape validation).
- Candle-direction / MACD-RSI-EMA confluence targets ("55%+ OOS") — unvalidated PRD aspiration.
- Python stack overall (nix/uv/py-clob-client) — non-portable to TS/Rust runtime.

### One-line verdict
Concept donor for the C-200 copy lane — its *gates* (0.55–0.80 fill band, 20s delay, ≤15% move, spread/depth filters), *delay-cost model*, and *BUY-only + per-wallet-per-market dedupe* are the transferable heuristics, and its honest WS-stub shows per-wallet detection is REST-poll-bound today — but nothing is licensed, nothing is calibrated, and the streak "570-market" rate table is an unverified claim to re-derive on the PMA tape.

## 3) tradinglabpremium / sports-prediction-market-scanner

### What it is
TypeScript library + CLI (~2.5k LOC) branded "Soccer Edge Market Scanner (SEMS)" — a **faithful TS port of the Python `georgedouzas/sports-betting` example stack** (the README's own "Type mapping (vs toolbox)" table maps SampleLeagueFeed→DummySoccerDataLoader, MarketEdgePricer→OddsComparisonBettor, TemporalCv→TimeSeriesSplit, AbstractFeed→BaseDataLoader — renamed domain types, openly documented). MIT (c) 2024 one-line license. Single git commit ("update original insert"); CHANGELOG padded with fake "maintenance patch 1..24" rows. **Despite the repo name: zero prediction-market wiring anywhere** — no Polymarket/Kalshi API, no market-data source, no order/CLOB concepts; it scans *bookmaker odds* (williamhill/interwetten) for value vs multi-book consensus.

### Verified content-or-machinery (with file paths)
- `pricing/marketEdgePricer.ts` (87 ln) — **exists and computes real math**: implied prob per leg = `1/(mean odds across selected books) − alpha` (alpha default 0.05, CLI uses 0.03); `basePricer.ts` renormalizes over complementary event sets (1X2; over/under 2.5/3.5) and `bet()` fires when `p·odds > 1`, selecting only the **max-expected-return leg within each complementary group** (no contradictory double-bets). README claim "compares mean implied probabilities against posted odds" is accurate as implemented.
- `feeds/sampleLeagueFeed.ts` (339 ln) — **canned 12-row in-memory table** (Greece/Spain/England/France, 1997–2019 + two synthetic "fixtures" rows dated today+2), mirroring the Python package's dummy data (same Olympiakos/Panathinaikos rows). Real row/odds shapes, nulls, param grid validation.
- `feeds/githubSoccerFeed.ts` (166 ln) — **real remote data**: fetches georgedouzas sports-betting modelling CSVs (historical soccer odds/targets 2020 England/Spain/Italy/Germany/France E1/E2) + fixtures.csv from raw.githubusercontent.com; cached (Redis or memory). **Dead in the CLI** — `scanner/run.ts` only ever instantiates SampleLeagueFeed; GithubSoccerFeed reachable only via library API.
- `scanner/run.ts` (179 ln) — CLI: `feed schema/books/pull`, `book replay` (walk-forward backtest w/ TemporalCv(2) over williamhill odds), `book signals` (value bets on the 2 canned fixture rows), `vault ping/purge`. Hardcodes oddsType=williamhill, alpha=0.03, splits=2. Redis optional w/ memory fallback.
- `portfolio/backtestRunner.ts` (80 ln) + `temporalCv.ts` — proper temporal walk-forward splitter, per-fold train/test date ranges, per-market bet counts + yield + ROI rows; `score()` = annualized-Sharpe-like mean/std × √365 of daily grouped returns.
- `__tests__/dummy.test.ts`, `redisCache.test.ts` — structural/plumbing tests (shapes, column-name conventions, cache keys): pass conceptually but **never assert predictive quality**; no accuracy/edge assertion exists anywhere.
- `core/frameKit.ts`, `core/checks.ts`, `core/schema.ts`, `feeds/abstractFeed.ts` — competent small dataframe-ish layer (schema validation, NA-dropping thresholds, param-grid conformance) — signs of a careful port, not scribbled scaffold. `pricing/modelPricer.ts` — stub wrapper around a generic `Classifier` interface with **no classifier implementation shipped** (dead abstraction).

### Verdict: real code vs AI-generated/renamed scaffold
**REAL CODE, REBRANDED + HALF-UNWIRED, MISLEADING NAME.** The pricer/backtest/frame math is real and internally consistent (verified: de-vig-by-subtraction + complementary-set normalization + p·odds>1 + single-leg-per-group selection; walk-forward splits are honest temporal CV). It is NOT a hallucinated scaffold — but it is (a) a renamed port of an existing open library's demo layer (SampleLeagueFeed dummy data proves lineage), (b) wired only to canned data in the CLI (GithubSoccerFeed is dead code; "live signals" come from 2 synthetic fixture rows), (c) **not a prediction-market scanner in any functional sense** — "sports-prediction-market" branding is aspirational; honest name is soccer bookmaker-odds value scanner. Tests are non-tautological but content-free (structure only); changelog/commit history smell cosmetic.

### Testable-hypothesis / transfer list (with effort estimate)
1. **Consensus-vs-posted-price value gate → Data-1 sports lane concept (MED effort).** Their decision logic maps cleanly: model/consensus implied prob vs posted price, fire when `p·odds > 1` (or ≥ margin), renormalize across complementary outcomes (1X2 / O2.5U2.5), never bet two legs of one complementary group. On Polymarket: p = v39/consensus prior, odds = 1/market price → the transferable H is *"Polymarket soccer 1X2 price vs external closing-odds consensus deviates >X bps often enough to matter"*; test occurrence rate on tape/live book prices before building anything. Effort: 1–2 days measurement.
2. **De-vig method comparison (LOW effort).** Their `1/odds − alpha` (fixed subtraction) vs proper margin-normalization (Σ-weights) vs stack's calibrated engine: which implied-prob extraction tracks resolution best on soccer markets — cheap calibration study on tape. Effort: <1 day.
3. **Temporal walk-forward + per-market yield reporting (already doctrine).** Their per-fold, per-market yield/ROI/bet-count reporting table is a decent reporting template if the stack's shadow reports lack per-market yield splits. Effort: N/A unless gap confirmed.
4. **Real historical soccer odds source (LOW effort).** georgedouzas/sports-betting CSVs (football-data.co.uk lineage, 1997–2020) = free historical bookmaker-consensus dataset usable offline to backtest "would consensus-closing-odds have beaten Polymarket prices" once PMA/live soccer prices exist. Effort: <0.5 day to vendor data.
5. **Beta/backlog hygiene antipattern (process).** Dead exports (GithubSoccerFeed, ModelPricer/Classifier, loadDataLoader), single commit, cosmetic changelog, structural-only tests = a live example of "rename-and-publish" audit smell to recognize in future triage (check wiring of every export to a CLI path).

### NOT-usable list
- As a prediction-market scanner: nothing connects to a prediction market (no feeds, no APIs, no market semantics).
- SampleLeagueFeed rows and its "signals" output (2 synthetic fixtures) — demo only; any backtest on it is meaningless.
- CLI as shipped (hardcoded williamhill/alpha/splits; dummy-only data path).
- modelPricer/Classifier abstraction (unimplemented); saveBettor/loadBettor throw "not implemented"; Redis layer irrelevant.
- No performance claims exist to falsify — nothing to shadow-test as an alpha source.

### One-line verdict
Real, well-ported bookmaker value-betting machinery wearing a misleading prediction-market name: usable as a compact *reference implementation* for consensus-odds de-vig + complementary-group value gating + temporal walk-forward (concepts only), but it contributes no Polymarket machinery, no live feed, and no validated edge — Data-1 lane value is limited to the "external-odds consensus vs Polymarket price deviation" measurement idea and the free georgedouzas odds dataset.
