## The two questions, and what the numbers said

**Q1 — "Should we bring $500/day down to $250/day and tighten the tuning?"**
The level was never the problem: the **test** was. Under "7 consecutive days each ≥ target", $250 also
fails (1/7), so lowering the number alone converts a 0/7 into a 1/7 and unblocks nothing.

**Q2 — "How does the $500/day look when we factor Kalshi in?"**
It changes by **$0.00** — Kalshi was never *in* the number, and the report line that makes it look like
it is, is an identity by construction.

## Key insight

The book earns a **run-rate** while almost never posting 7 individually-qualifying days. The gate was
therefore measuring a day-to-day consistency this book structurally does not have — and no threshold
tuning can fix a mis-specified test. Replacing "each of 7 days ≥ target" with "the 7-day SUM ≥ 7 ×
target" keeps the identical 7-day ambition and makes the ladder advance again.

## Kalshi: why it is $0.00, and why it stays out of the goal

- `kalshi-shadow-summary.json` is declared **counterfactual attribution** — rows are priced at the
  Polymarket reference because live Kalshi routing is retired. Its `live.pnl` is **$5,729.99** and settled
  C-200 realized is **$5,729.99** — identical to the cent, i.e. it is the same book at the same prices.
  The "N consecutive windows agree" line the reviews print is a **reconciliation check**, not a second
  venue earning the same money.
- The one instrument that prices Kalshi's own book (`kalshi-venue-shadow`, forward-only, paper):
  **789 in-window copies → 2 with a Kalshi quote = 0.3% cross-list**, 707 quote failures. On those 2 legs
  PM +$29.27 vs Kalshi +$0.17. n=2 is not a sample.
- The 0.3% is structural, not a matcher bug: against Kalshi's complete index (14,766 events) **0 of our
  202 in-window markets clear 0.60** match confidence.
- No Kalshi copy lane either: the venue gate is unsatisfiable by construction (pre-boost composite ≥
  87.33 paired with `copyScore ≥ 80` post-boost, against a sentiment clamp at 79). 0 trades since
  2026-08-30 and 0 of 335 C-200 copies since Sep 5.

**Verdict: Kalshi is excluded from the goal until something prices ≥ 50 legs.** Recorded as a decision,
not as a code change — nothing in the goal ever read Kalshi.

## What changed (before → after)

- **Ladder:** `[500, 1000, 2000, 5000]` → **`250, 500, 1000, 2000, 5000`** (doubling retained; $500 becomes
  Phase 2 with its basis stated honestly, 0/61 windows).
- **Advance basis:** "7 consecutive settled days each ≥ target" → **"the trailing 7 SETTLED days SUM ≥
  target × 7"**.
- **Versioning:** every report now prints `Goal policy: v2-rolling-7d-sum` so any read can name which
  ladder *and* which basis produced it (the same discipline as `ruleSetVersion`).
- **The old basis is not deleted:** the consecutive-day streak is still computed and printed, explicitly
  labelled **REPORTED ONLY, NOT the advance basis** — the daily reviews quote it and it remains the
  day-to-day consistency instrument.
- **Dashboard:** the ladder and the gate card moved with the report (a page that contradicts the report
  is worse than no page). The overview's ladder tick marks are now **derived** from `C200_PHASES`; they
  had been hardcoded at 10/20/40/100% while the bar was scaled to the *active* target, so every tick
  mislabelled the bar.
- **Analytics page:** same ladder/basis, and its closing paragraph no longer says "N more for the gate"
  (it now reads the rolling-7d sum against the rung requirement).

## Reality checks / caveats

- **This changes a PUBLISHED NUMBER** (the gate the ladder advances on), which is why it needed the
  approval it got — it is a measurement change, not a rule change.
- The apparent magnitude matters: the current settled window is **short by $41.22**, i.e. Phase 1 is now a
  reachable rung and one good settled day could clear it. Clearance is still judged on **settled** days
  (T+3), never on today's provisional bucket.
- The 13% (8/61) hit rate at $250/day is a *historical* rate on a book whose regime has changed (fill
  model 2026-08-28, v60/v61/v62 sizing). It is the right shape for a milestone gate; it is not a
  projection.
- The dashboard's own gate reads are **T+0 and row-scoped** (`resolvedAt ?? openedAt`) and will not match
  the report's settled line — both pages now say so on their face.

   snap change.
